# Xpose

**X Accounts. Xposed.**

*One handle. Many faces. All documented.*

A free browser extension that helps you see which Twitter/X accounts have been flagged by the community. Know who you're engaging with before you follow or share.

🌐 **Website:** [xpose.world](https://xpose.world)

---

## What Does It Do?

When you browse Twitter/X, Xpose shows you:

- 🏷️ **Badges** next to flagged accounts (like "Paid Promoter" or "Propaganda")
- 🔗 **Link to profile** showing all community reports about that account
- 🚩 **Report button** to flag suspicious accounts yourself

---

## How to Install (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Download

👉 [Click here to download](https://github.com/xxddgghh/twitter-handle-flagger/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)

### Step 2: Unzip the file

- Find the downloaded file (usually in your Downloads folder)
- Right-click and select "Extract All" or double-click to unzip

### Step 3: Add to Chrome

1. Open Chrome and go to: `chrome://extensions`
2. Turn ON **"Developer mode"** (switch in top-right corner)
3. Click **"Load unpacked"**
4. Select the folder you just unzipped

### Step 4: Done!

Go to [twitter.com](https://twitter.com) or [x.com](https://x.com) - the extension works automatically.

## Categories

| Badge | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| 🟡 **Paid Promoter** | Promotes products/services without telling you they're paid |
| 🔴 **Propaganda** | Spreads misleading information or narratives |
| 🟤 **Brown Sepoy** | Indians working against Indian interests |
| ⬛ **Liar / Hypocrite** | Says different things at different times |
| 🟠 **Anti-India** | Promotes anti-India content |
| 🟧 **Pro-Bharat** | Talks about Indian interests and nationalism |
| ⚫ **Bot/Automated** | Likely a bot, not a real person |

## How to Report an Account

See a suspicious account? Here's how to report:

1. **Find their tweet** on Twitter/X
2. **Click the 🚩 flag icon** on the tweet
3. **Pick a category** that fits
4. **Add evidence** (optional but helpful)
5. **Click Submit** - it opens GitHub where you confirm

That's it! Your report helps the community.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this free?**
Yes, completely free.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Not yet - only on Chrome browser (computer).

**Can I get someone wrongly flagged?**
Reports need verification. One person can't flag someone alone.

**Is my data safe?**
The extension only reads Twitter pages. It doesn't access your account or personal data.

**How do I remove it?**
Go to `chrome://extensions`, find "Xpose", click Remove.
